# Project checkpoint — 2026-10-26

## Current work

- **P0:** M0–M8 are complete. INV-04 remains active after ADR-0027's accepted
  within-group heat mixing and shared radiation refit (τ_d = 1.147,
  κ_v = 0.005735 m²/kg). The L4 three-year V8 global gate passes at
  reference minus climate −0.029 K and a reference/climate precipitation
  ratio of 0.908; regional land/ocean differences remain. The next proposed
  decision concerns tropical heat exchange, not M9. See INV-04 and ADR-0027
  for the runs and gates.
- **Rendering/UI:** the climate-lab drawer separates Help, Controls, Inspect
  and Log; the live solar factor goes through PlanetRun's validated command
  queue. Captures save a PNG and view metadata; the orbit/coast/local series
  holds one focus and simulation time, can be cancelled, and restores the
  camera. Ocean motion is scale-filtered and cloud/land shading was adjusted
  without changing any solver or PSNAP field.
- **Open rendering decision:** R1-05 is proposed, not implemented. At the
  filtered-to-detailed terrain handoff, the L4 seed-20260928 probe found
  100/532 coastal samples (18.8%) switch land/water classification. An
  ADR-0022 amendment must choose a coast/LOD representation and numeric
  displacement gate before changing the tile generator.
- **Other proposals:** M8A's physical cloud-water advection needs an accepted
  ADR resolving ADR-0023 §8. The new `docs/references.md` and
  `docs/state-of-the-art-procedural-planets.md` collect research; literature
  is not an implementation decision.

## Checks and limitations

- Godot extension builds; headless UI/solar-command smoke, X11 capture series,
  style readability, detail coverage and terrain-relief/tile tests pass in
  the local runs. A short L4 tile flight found zero holes and under 0.00006 px
  camera-relative translation rounding at 100 m. The X11/Intel timings are
  diagnostic, not the §31.9 reference-hardware performance gate.
- `godot/tests/test_clouds.gd` failed at its first cloud-layer assertion
  with newly generated L2 one- and three-year recordings; the cause remains
  uninvestigated. Do not weaken the test to close this item. An L4 live
  cloud-focused capture after twelve months did draw the cloud layer, so the
  L2 recording/test path needs a separate diagnosis.
- The terrain shading improvement and close-range ocean texture do **not**
  fix the coastline jump; ×25 relief remains the default because lowering it
  increased local tile/cache pressure in the short flight. ×8 is still an
  opt-in view control.
